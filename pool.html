Launch the trading pool • Aerodrome on Base

What a pool is, in one breath: a little two-sided shop window. One side holds BC, the other side holds ETH. That pair gives BC its first price — and lets anybody buy or sell BC, any time, without asking anyone.

What goes in (your plan):
• 500,000,000 BC — half your supply (you keep the other 500M)
• 0.00089 ETH — about $2.01
• Starting price: 1 BC ≈ $0.0000000040

You get pool receipt tokens (LP) back in your wallet — they prove the pool money is yours and let you pull it out later. You can add more to the pool any time; small pool = small trades only for now, and price can swing hard — that’s normal at this size.
